# On-call

## Before your rotation

- Confirm you can receive pages on your phone.
- Skim open incidents and recent deploys.
- Check that your VPN and break-glass access work.

## Escalation

| Tier | Who | Page after |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | You | immediately |
| Secondary | Backup on-call | 30 minutes unacked |
| Manager | EM | 60 minutes unacked |

If you cannot make progress in 30 minutes, escalate. Escalation is not failure;
it is the system working as designed.

## Handoff

At the end of your rotation, write a two-line summary of anything still smoldering
and tag the next on-call.
